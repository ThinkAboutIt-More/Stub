# Watchlist Ideas Backlog

Feature candidates. Nothing here ships without Ary's explicit yes. When he picks one, it moves into PUNCHLIST.md and gets built.

## Floated by Ary / Alpha (Aug 30, 2026)

- **Opening-night showtimes link** — when a watchlist title hits theaters, one tap to showtimes near him.
- **Post-stub-scan rating nudge** — after scanning a ticket stub, prompt for his rating once he's had time to see it.
- **Spoiler-shield auto-mute** — anything in the watchlist he hasn't seen yet gets spoilers suppressed everywhere (reason lines, fan text, searches).
- **Wrapped-style year in review** — his year of watching: stats, streaks, top genres, hidden gems the crowd loved that he skipped.
- **Taste overlap for watching with someone** — pick a title two people will both like, based on both ratings histories.

## Alpha candidates (unvetted)

- **Rewatch radar** — flag old favorites he rated high and hasn't logged in years.
- **Streaming availability alerts** — notify when a watchlist title lands on a service he has (JustWatch/TMDB watch-provider data, no key cost).
- **One-tap "rate it" from notifications** — log a rating without opening the app.
- **Friend recs inbox** — someone texts him a title, it lands in a "Suggested by friends" shelf with their name attached.
- **Mood filter** — "something short", "something heavy", filter watchlist by runtime/tone signals.
- **Stats shelf** — hours watched, rating distribution, genre fingerprint; living stats, not just yearly.
- **Release-day countdown** — days-until badges on Coming Soon cards.
- **Trailer shelf** — inline trailers on title detail (YouTube embeds via TMDB).
- **"Where do I know them from"** — spoiler-safe cast cross-reference, only for titles already logged/seen.
