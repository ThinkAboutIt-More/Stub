# Watchlist Ideas Backlog

Feature candidates and verdicts. Nothing ships without Ary's explicit yes. Approved items move into the build queue; rejected ones stay here as a record of the no.

## APPROVED - build queue (verdicts Aug 30, 2026)

- **Opening-night mode** — watchlist film hits theaters, one tap to showtimes near him. APPROVED.
- **Spoiler shield** — auto-mute plot keywords for watchlist titles he hasn't seen yet, everywhere in the app. APPROVED ("huge"). Design rule: never surface the keyword list itself.
- **Year in review AND month in review** — Wrapped-style recap of his ratings, theaters, most-watched directors; yearly + monthly editions. APPROVED ("love").
- **Juicier buttons + more responsive, fun animations** — his words: "more responsive and fun." Applies to audit item #19; raise the ceiling beyond the v52 pass. APPROVED.
- **Post-viewing rating nudge (data-driven, NO scanning)** — he rejected ticket scanning; instead, use signals already available (confirmation emails, AMC/Regal activity) to know what he saw and when it ended, then nudge for the rating after the movie. Design call on delivery: in-app prompt vs Alpha texts him. APPROVED as the scanning replacement.
- **Algorithm direction (his spec, Aug 30, 2026)** — one single legible blended percentage: HIS taste + HIS ratings + audience scores, that actually re-adjusts as he keeps rating and watching. Blend weights keep re-learning with every new rating. This governs the Phase-1 algorithm build.

## REJECTED - record of the no

- **Ticket scanning flow** — "I don't really want to get in the process of scanning the tickets." Replaced by the data-driven rating nudge above.
- **Friend/taste-overlap feature** — "I don't care about who I'm watching it with."

## Alpha candidates (unvetted - pending his yes)

- **Rewatch radar** — flag old favorites he rated high and hasn't logged in years.
- **Streaming availability alerts** — notify when a watchlist title lands on a service he has (free via TMDB watch providers).
- **One-tap "rate it" from notifications** — log a rating without opening the app.
- **Friend recs inbox** — someone texts him a title, it lands in a "Suggested by friends" shelf with their name attached.
- **Mood filter** — "something short", "something heavy", filter watchlist by runtime/tone signals.
- **Stats shelf** — hours watched, rating distribution, genre fingerprint; living stats, not just yearly.
- **Release-day countdown** — days-until badges on Coming Soon cards.
- **Trailer shelf** — inline trailers on title detail (YouTube embeds via TMDB).
- **"Where do I know them from"** — spoiler-safe cast cross-reference, only for titles already logged/seen.
